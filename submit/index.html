Rules

Member sites must:

  • support plain old unsecured, unencrypted HTTP.
    • This excludes most popular free webhosts (eg Neocities, Nekoweb). There are other options.
  • be written by a human, at least partially in English.
  • be curl-able (without any flags).

Member sites must not:

  • require JavaScript for basic functionality, including forcing the visitor to complete a JS-based CAPTCHA or PoW (eg Anubis, Cloudflare).
  • discriminate against old user agents, which defeats the purpose.
    • This excludes many popular hosts that support HTTP (eg AlwaysData, Bear Blog).
  • espouse fascism, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or any other form of bigotry or right-wing ideology.

If you don't understand some of the terms used on this page, that's okay! As long as you can access your site at http://yoursite.com without being redirected to https://yoursite.com, just submit it and I'll let you know if it qualifies.
